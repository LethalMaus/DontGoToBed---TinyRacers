# Voiceover scripts

Read naturally, with a smile; leave the gaps rather than filling every second.
Timings refer to the supplied edits. They already have a quiet music bed. Record
one WAV per video at 48 kHz if convenient. No need to imitate a trailer announcer.
The gameplay is real emulator footage with scripted controllers; the controller
capture uses synthetic motion. Neither is footage of the boys playing.

## Recording and finishing

Use headphones so the backing track doesn't leak into your recording. Keep your
microphone position steady and record in a quiet room. Record the timed sections
as separate takes if that is easier; leave each at its listed start time in your
editor. The supplied picture-only MP4s are clean of music but retain text labels.
The voiceover-ready MP4s have an edited music bed; they contain no recorded speech.

The scripts are not subtitles yet: captions must follow the voice you actually
record. Edit the words freely, then adjust cuts/captions to the final take.
