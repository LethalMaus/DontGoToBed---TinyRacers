Your phone becomes your steering wheel. Don’t Go To Bed: Tiny Racers brings the family together for a race on the big screen.

Race around the dining table, through the garden and across the bedroom with the characters from our family’s Don’t Go To Bed game. Collect toy power-ups, hit ramps and booster pads, and try to stay with the pack. Family bragging rights are at stake.

Play solo, add computer opponents or race with up to four players. Phones and tablets connect through their browsers, with no controller app to install. Automatic acceleration, automatic items and adjustable steering help younger players get started.

Built by James Cullimore for the Fire TV track of the Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon. This is a working prototype, demonstrated in the Android TV emulator. Android Chrome and iPad Safari have also been tested as controllers. Physical Fire TV testing and an Appstore release are still ahead. The current hosted connection requires internet.
