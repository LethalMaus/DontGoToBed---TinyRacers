# Tiny Racers media

[Watch the approved hackathon demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCKjIHIIFqA).

- `exports/devpost-gallery/`: eight numbered screenshots, captions and a visual guide.
- `exports/artwork/`: approved thumbnails, promotional layouts and video cards.
- `exports/screenshots/`: current gameplay and controller screenshots.
- `VOICEOVER_SCRIPTS.md` and `YOUTUBE_COPY.md`: recording scripts and upload copy.
- `source/` and `scripts/`: code-based layouts and video editing recipes.

The demo is 2:30 and has the owner’s approved narration. The optional 1:00 trailer
and 1:59 tutorial picture edits and music beds are prepared locally; their separate
voiceovers are not yet complete. Video masters and private recordings are excluded
from Git. The current gallery uses the approved character artwork and hosted lobby.

Gameplay is captured from the Android TV emulator using scripted controllers.
Controller walkthroughs use synthetic motion. They are demonstrations of the
software, not recordings of physical family playtests. See [credits](../CREDITS.md)
and [asset terms](../ASSET_LICENSE.md). Do not redistribute music as standalone files.
